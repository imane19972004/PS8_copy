How to Play

🎯 Objective

Destroy your opponent's Pharaoh with your laser beam while protecting your own.

⚡ Actions

Each turn: rotate a piece, move it, place a pyramid, or swap your Scarab.

💥 Laser

After your action, your Sphinx fires. The laser reflects on mirrors and destroys vulnerable pieces.

🏆 Victory

Hit the enemy Pharaoh to win. Protect yours at all costs!

The Pieces

🗿

Sphinx

Immobile laser source. Indestructible.

👑

Pharaoh

Immobile. Lose if destroyed.

🐺

Anubis

Mobile. 1 shielded side.

🔺

Pyramid

Mobile mirror. 2 reflective sides.

🪲

Scarab

Mobile. Double mirror. Can swap.

About Khet

Khet is a modern abstract strategy game combining lasers with ancient Egyptian themes. This digital adaptation was created as part of the PS8 Full-Stack Developer course at Polytech Nice Sophia.

Technologies: Node.js, Socket.io, Canvas API, Pure JavaScript (ES6+)
